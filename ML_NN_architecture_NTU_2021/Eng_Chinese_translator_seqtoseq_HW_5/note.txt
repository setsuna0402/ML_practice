This is a sequence to sequence model with transformer encoder and decoder.
This work builds a English-to-Chinese word translator.
Validation way: BLEU score with 1/2/3/4-gram precision.
Training: Teacher forcing 
Beam-search for influence (Beam width = 5)
** hypothesis is the model predicted sentence. reference is the correct sentence.
Example for n-gram:
Original: 我喜歡貓咪
1-gram: 我 喜 歡 貓 咪
2-gram: 我喜 喜歡 歡貓 貓咪
3-gram: 我喜歡 喜歡貓 歡貓咪
4-gram: 我喜歡貓 喜歡貓咪
Meaning: 
1-gram checks whether the key words included in the hypothesis.
2-gram checks phase and word collocation
3-gram checks short/locat structure of the sentence
4-gram checks sentence fluency/gramma/global meaning
high n-gram precision => better grammar, phrase ordering and looks like the reference
but the correct rate for high n-gram is unusally lower.
** BLEU score = BP * exp(1/N sum_{n=1}^{N}(log(p_n))), here p_n is the accuracy of the n-gram.
** n = 1 to 4 in this work.
** BP = 1 if c>=r else exp(1 - r/c) here r is the length of reference and c is the length of generated translation

The BLEU score of the trained model (30 epoch): about 23
