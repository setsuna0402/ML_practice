This project is a implementation of LLM's fine tuning.
We adopt Qwen2.5-3B model as the pretrained model, 
and use Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) method to train a Chinese poem writter.
To achieve this with the limited computation instrucment (one RTX 3060 Ti),
we use 4-bit quantization method with NF4 format to represent the pretrained model, 
while all caculation is done in bf16. Also, we switch to Qwen2.5-3B model from TAIDE-LX-7B-Chat, which is used in the NTU example code.
The results look OK even the number of parameters of Qwen2.5 is only half of TAIDE-LX-7B-Chat.
(Please be aware that I am a PhD in physics, not literary.)
At least the format of most of the results is consistent and the poem is fluent. 

These results show that my understanding on fine tuning is correct and the customized input-output functions (generate_training_data and evaluate) which are designed for Qwen2.5 work correctly.
